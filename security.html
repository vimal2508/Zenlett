SAFETY FIRST

Security & user safety

Verify every provider before sharing information or making a payment.

Protect your account

  • Use a unique, strong password.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or PINs.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Be cautious of urgent payment requests.

Verify third parties

  • Use official websites and contact details.
  • Check registration and licensing where applicable.
  • Read fees, refund rules and privacy terms.
  • Do not rely on guaranteed-profit claims.

Digital assets

Digital-asset services can involve substantial risk. Confirm provider, network, wallet address, fees and applicable laws independently.

Report a concern

Contact Zenlet about suspicious listings. Never send passwords, OTPs or private keys.
