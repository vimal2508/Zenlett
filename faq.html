Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using the Zenlet directory.

What is Zenlet?

Zenlet is a service-discovery and referral platform that helps users find independent providers and their official websites.

Does Zenlet provide the listed services?

Services are provided by independent third parties unless a page clearly states otherwise.

Does Zenlet guarantee income or returns?

No. Zenlet does not guarantee profits, income, returns, commissions or results.

How do I check a provider?

Verify its official website, business details, licensing where applicable, fees and refund policy.

How can I contact Zenlet?

Use the contact page. Do not include passwords, OTPs or private keys.
